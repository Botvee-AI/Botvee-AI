## What Botvee does

Botvee gives your business an AI agent that answers customers, sells your products and hands the hard cases to your team, on every channel your customers already use.

- **Answers in seconds** from your own website, products and documents
- **Sells**, recommending your best sellers and bringing back abandoned carts
- **Confirms Cash on Delivery orders** on WhatsApp before they ship
- **Tracks parcels** in the chat, across 3,500+ couriers
- **Books meetings**, captures leads and opens support tickets
- **Hands off to a human** with the full context, in one shared Inbox

## Use Botvee from your AI assistant

Botvee runs a secure MCP server, so you can manage your workspace from the assistant you already use: read live conversations, leads and tickets, and make changes you approve.

**Claude** (listed in Claude's connector directory as *Botvee AI Agent*) · **ChatGPT** · **Gemini** · **Grok**
